About Lucky Shrub

Based in Tuscon, Arizona, Lucky Shrub is a medium-sized garden design firm that specializes in garden design/creation, maintenance, and landscaping. We also run a plant nursery with a wide selection of both outdoor and indoor plants.


Lucky Shrub was founded in 2010 by Jason and Maria, who have a passion for creating beautiful outdoor spaces. Jason is the garden architect, and Maria manages the nursery as well as marketing and customer relations. Together, they have built a team of talented designers and landscapers who share their vision for creating beautiful outdoor spaces.
